What is Ledger?

Ledger is a browser-based crypto portfolio tracker designed to give you one clear place to record your holdings, follow current market prices and understand portfolio performance.

It focuses on a simple experience: add the assets you own, enter your average buy price, and let the dashboard calculate current value and profit or loss using online market data.

What Ledger Does

  • Organizes your crypto holdings in one dashboard
  • Shows current market prices from an online market-data source
  • Calculates portfolio value and profit/loss
  • Lets you add and remove holdings without creating an account

Our Approach

We aim for a clean interface, understandable numbers and practical portfolio tracking without unnecessary complexity.

Ledger is a tracking tool, not a bank, exchange or broker. It does not execute trades or hold cryptocurrency for you.

Your Portfolio Data

Portfolio entries are stored in your browser so the tracker can work without an account. Your saved holdings can be affected if you clear browser storage, change devices or use private browsing.

Looking Ahead

Ledger can grow with features such as charts, transaction history, watchlists, exports and additional market-data tools while keeping the core experience straightforward.
