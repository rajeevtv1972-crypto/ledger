This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Ledger, a browser-based crypto portfolio tracking website.

Portfolio Data

Ledger does not require you to create an account. The portfolio entries you add in the tracker are stored in your browser's local storage so the site can remember them on that browser and device. Ledger's application does not send those portfolio entries to its database or use them to create an online user account.

Market Data

Ledger requests current cryptocurrency prices from an online market-data service. These requests contain the supported asset identifiers needed to retrieve prices. Market-data providers may process request information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current Ledger portfolio tracker does not use an account session or authentication cookie. Your browser may still retain normal site storage used by the application, including local storage containing your portfolio entries.

Third-Party Services

Links and online services used by Ledger may be operated by third parties. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies. Ledger is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites you choose to visit.

Children's Information

Ledger is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the service does not require an account, Ledger is not designed to request a child's personal profile information.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Ledger's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
